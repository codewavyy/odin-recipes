Mofongo

This is a famous dish in Puerto Rico

Ingredients:

  • Plantain
  • Salt
  • Oil
  • Protein
  • Garlic

How To Make Mofongo

  1. Cut the plantain in small portions.
  2. Put some oil in a pan and heat it on the stove over medium heat.
  3. Put the plantains in the pan and let them fry until they turn yellow.
  4. Take them out and put them in a mortar, then mash them until you get a moldable dough.
  5. Enjoy
