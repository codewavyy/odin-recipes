Arroz con Dulce

Ingredients:

  • Long-grain white rice: 1 cup (240 g)
  • Water: 2–3 cups (480–720 mL)
  • Whole milk: 3–4 cups (720–960 mL)
  • Evaporated milk: 1 can (12 oz / 355 mL)
  • Sweetened condensed milk: 1 can (14 oz / 397 mL)
  • Cinnamon sticks: 2 (3-inch / 7.5 cm)
  • Lime or orange zest: 1 small strip (optional, for citrus flavor)
  • Kosher salt: ⅛ tsp (1.5 g)
  • Ground cinnamon: For garnish (optional)

Step by Step:

  1. Rinse the rice under cold water until the water runs clear to remove excess starch.
  2. In a saucepan, combine the rinsed rice, water, cinnamon sticks, and a pinch of salt. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for 15–20 minutes until the water is absorbed and the rice is tender.
  3. In a separate bowl, warm the whole milk, evaporated milk, and condensed milk together over low heat or in a microwave until lukewarm.
  4. Remove the cinnamon sticks from the rice. Stir in the warmed milk mixture and sugar. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce heat to low.
  5. Simmer uncovered for 25–30 minutes, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking and scorching. The mixture should thicken to a creamy consistency. If it becomes too thick, add a splash of milk to adjust.
  6. Remove from heat. Stir in vanilla extract and optional citrus zest. Let cool slightly for 30 minutes before serving.
  7. Serve warm or chilled. Garnish each portion with a sprinkle of ground cinnamon.
